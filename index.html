Bio

I'm an third-year PhD student at (The Ackermann Research Group), Georg-August-Universität Göttingen. My research focuses on molecular machine learning and mechanistic investigations in asymmetric catalysis..

I received my Bachelor's degree in Honors Chemistry from University of Delhi. Then I did my Master's in Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore. During my Master's, I joined (Computational Materials Designing Group) under the supervision of Prof. Biswarup Pathak for my Master's thesis. There, I learned DFT calculations for periodic systems and machine learning applications. I developed an interest in data science applications for chemical space exploration.
