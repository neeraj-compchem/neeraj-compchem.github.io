Bio

I am a final-year PhD candidate in Chemistry at the Georg-August-Universität Göttingen, under the supervision of Prof. Lutz Ackermann. My PhD is generously supported by the DFG’s priority programme SPP 2363 on Molecular Machine Learning. My research lies at the intersection of computational chemistry, asymmetric catalysis, and machine learning. I am particularly interested in data-driven and DFT-based approaches to understand and predict reactivity and selectivity in 3d transition metal-catalyzed C–H activation reactions.

I received my BSc degree in Honors Chemistry from the University of Delhi (Rajdhani College), followed by a MSc in Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore. My master's thesis, advised by Prof. Biswarup Pathak, focused on the rational design of 3d metal HER catalysts using supervised machine learning.

Outside the lab, I enjoy traveling, trying new cuisines, exploring mountain regions, and landscape photography.
