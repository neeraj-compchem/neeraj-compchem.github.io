Bio

I'm a third-year PhD student at the Georg-August-Universität Göttingen, working with Prof. Lutz Ackermann. My research lies at the intersection of computational chemistry, asymmetric catalysis, and machine learning. I’m particularly interested in applying data-driven approaches to understand reactivity and selectivity trends, predict site selectivity in C–H activation, and quantify ring strain in strained molecules.

I received my Bachelor's degree in Honors Chemistry from University of Delhi, followed by a Master's in Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, where I conducted my thesis in the Computational Materials Designing Group led by Prof. Biswarup Pathak. There, I was introduced to periodic DFT calculations and early applications of machine learning in catalysis. This experience sparked my passion for integrating data science with mechanistic chemistry.

Outside the lab, I enjoy traveling, trying new cuisines, exploring mountain regions, and landscape photography.
