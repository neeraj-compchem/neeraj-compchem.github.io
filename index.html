Bio

I'm a third-year doctoral student in Chemistry at the Georg-August-Universität Göttingen, under the supervision of Prof. Lutz Ackermann. I received my bachelor's degree in Honors Chemistry from University of Delhi (Rajdhani College), followed by a master's in Chemistry from the Indian Institute of Technology Indore, where I wrote my thesis advised by Prof. Biswarup Pathak.

My research lies at the intersection of computational chemistry, asymmetric catalysis, and machine learning. I’m particularly interested in data-driven and DFT-based approaches to understand and predict reactivity and selectivity in 3d transition metal-catalyzed C–H activation. My growing interests also include the application of quantum computing to reaction mechanism exploration and catalyst design.

Outside the lab, I enjoy traveling, trying new cuisines, exploring mountain regions, and landscape photography.
